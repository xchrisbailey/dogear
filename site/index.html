For Grimmory · iPhone & iPad

Your library, dog‑eared right where you left it.

Dogear is a native app for reading ebooks and listening to audiobooks from your self-hosted Grimmory server. Download for offline, and pick up on your phone exactly where the web reader left off.

View on GitHub In development, not on the App Store yet
On the shelf

Everything in your Grimmory library, in one place

Books on the shelves, audiobooks in cassette cases, and what you were last reading face-out at the front.

Read

EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW3 & FB2

An open book on a desk, with pages that curl. Choose the font, and two pages side by side on iPad or in landscape. MOBI, AZW3 and FB2 are converted to EPUB on the device.

Listen

Audiobooks, done properly

Single M4B files or folders of tracks, with chapters, 0.75× to 3× speed, a sleep timer that can stop at the end of a chapter, lock screen controls and AirPlay.

Sync

Progress that follows you

Dogear reads and writes the same position as Grimmory's web reader, down to the page. Start on your laptop, keep going on your phone.

Offline

Downloads for the commute

Keep any book or audiobook on the device. Progress you make offline waits in an outbox and goes up when you're back, and newer reading elsewhere always wins.

Connect

Made for a home server

Sign in with your Grimmory account over HTTPS, or plain HTTP to a server on your Tailscale network, which already encrypts the connection.

Log

Hardcover, hands-off

Grimmory already sends progress to Hardcover, so Dogear never asks for a Hardcover token. Read, and your reading log keeps itself.

Appearance

Three looks to choose from

Each theme dresses the library, the reader's chrome and the pocket tape player, which has its own machine in every one. Switch any time in Settings.

Walnut & Paper

The default. A paper header over a walnut cabinet, and a cream player on a leather strap.

Oak & Daylight

Light paper, honey oak shelves and sage controls, with a player under a carry handle.

Bookcloth & Tape

Woven deep teal, a cream Continue panel and orange accents, with a player on a lanyard.

What you need

Requirements

  • An iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 or later.
  • A Grimmory server you can sign in to. Dogear is tested against Grimmory 3.5.
  • The server reachable over HTTPS, or over HTTP on your Tailscale network.
Where it's at

Status

  • Dogear is a personal project by one developer, built in the open.
  • It isn't on the App Store yet. To try it now, build it from source with Xcode; the README has the steps.
